Enable Cloud Sync

Enabling Cloud Sync links your local study progress to a secure online database. Here is what will happen:

  • Data Merging: Your local progress (completed sets, starred cards) will merge with any data already in the cloud under your sync key (keeping the highest progress).
  • Missed Words: Any words in your "Missed Words" list will be combined with the cloud list so you don't lose any practice history.
  • Automatic Updates: Your progress will automatically sync in the background whenever you hide/close the app or exit study sessions.

To link another device's progress, paste your existing key here. Otherwise, use the dice to roll a new unique key.
